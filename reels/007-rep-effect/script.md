# 007 · The Rep effect

Our take on the "girlfriend effect" trend (a girlfriend posts her boyfriend before and after her). Kayla posts
Brody's glow-up and takes the credit; Rep cuts in with the receipts (the streak). It reuses the day-1 and day-30
Brody from `004-day-30`, so it is cheap once that one is drawn. About 18 s, 1080 x 1920, cut on the beat.
Cast: Kayla + Brody + Rep. See `../research.md` for why this format.

## Facts (checked in the FaceRep repo)

- Fish Face, Cheek Lift and Chin Lift are free (`FREE_EXERCISE_IDS`).
- "30 day streak" is the app's own wording (`home.json`); a free user keeps a streak with free exercises.

## Where the line is

- What changes between the photos: hair, clothes, posture, sleep (eyes open). His face's shape is drawn the same.
- Rep's receipt is the habit (days and exercises), never a result. Nothing says the face changed.
- The "before" is sleepy and messy, never ugly: no insult, no looks rating, nobody "mogged".
- No phone screen drawn: the "before" is a printed photo.
