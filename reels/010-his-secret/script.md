## Facts (checked in the FaceRep repo)

- Fish Face, Chin Lift and Brow Lift are free exercises (`FREE_EXERCISE_IDS`, `app/src/constants/exercises.ts`).
  Chin Lift is the free jawline one and has no jaw caution.
- Fish Face: 8 reps, hold 5 s. Its first step (`locales/en/exercises.json`): "Suck your cheeks in between your teeth."
- Chin Lift: tilt your head back, push your lower lip up over your top lip. Brow Lift: lift your eyebrows high.
- No result is shown or promised: Leo's face never changes.
- Safe, still moments only: at home, sitting after the gym, a park bench, his room. Normal US hours.
