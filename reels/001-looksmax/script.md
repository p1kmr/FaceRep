# Reel 001: looksmaxxing, but make it a workout

10 seconds, 1080 x 1920, for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. No voice. The sound is a phonk-style beat
made in code (128 BPM), and every cut lands on a beat (one shot = 4 beats = 1.9 s). The screens are the store
capture's Simulator screenshots (a free user with demo data), in a phone that moves; one detail of each screen is
lifted out, as on the store slides.

| Time | Screen | Words on screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.9 s | Chin Lift (Jawline) exercise page, rising from the bottom on dark red | LOOKSMAXXING · BUT MAKE IT A · **WORKOUT** (one per beat) | deep hit, the beat starts at once |
| 1.9–3.8 s | The same page; the red neck muscle lifted out in a circle | SEE THE MUSCLE · YOU TRAIN | whoosh into the cut |
| 3.8–5.6 s | Workout player (Brow Lift); the rep ring lifted out | SQUEEZE · HOLD · RELAX · **REPEAT** (one word per beat) | each word on a beat |
| 5.6–7.5 s | Progress; the streak card lifted out | DON'T BREAK · THE STREAK | ding when the card pops |
| 7.5–10 s | App icon | YOUR FACE · **HAS A GYM NOW** · FACEREP · Lips, Cheeks & Mewing Workouts · ON THE APP STORE | deep hit, last hit at 9.4 s |

## Why it is worded this way

- Gen Z words: looksmaxxing (the hook), mewing (end card, from the subtitle), streak, "has a gym now".
- The store text's rules (docs/store-listing.md): no result promised (no "sharper jawline in 7 days"), no
  before/after, no price, no other app. No shot is about the face scan: in a looksmaxxing reel it would read as a
  rating of looks, and it measures movement only. Its chart shows small on the Progress screen, under its real
  labels (Movement, Balance).
- The hook is never an insult ("you're mid"): Meta refuses ads that play on how people see themselves, so the same
  reel can be boosted later.
