# What goes viral in looksmax and glow-up videos (checked 2026-11-25)

A web search of news coverage and creator case studies (sources at the end). We are on Instagram Reels and YouTube
Shorts; most of these clips started on TikTok and spread from there.

## What we found

- **It is huge, and platforms are pushing back.** TikTok's own numbers (in Time): looksmaxxing searches went from
  300,000 a day in February 2026 to 1.9 million a day in March. TikTok then blocked searches for the phrase from
  April 3. YouTube closed channels of the best-known looksmax streamer. No Instagram block was found.
- **The glow-up format.** A rough "before" and a polished "after", with a hard cut between them on the beat drop. A
  hand swiping across the camera at about 3 s is the moment people rewatch. Captions: "How it started vs how it
  ended", "POV: …", "2020 vs 2026". The change has to be big and obvious; a small one doesn't hold attention.
- **What actually changes in viral glow-ups** is mostly the haircut, clothes, skincare and grooming. In the
  "girlfriend effect" trend (a girlfriend posts her boyfriend before and after her; one hit 10 million views) the
  coverage says that in most cases it comes down to a haircut.
- **Softmaxxing vs hardmaxxing.** Softmaxxing is the safe side: skincare, sleep, posture, a good haircut, dressing
  well, exercise. Hardmaxxing is steroids, surgery, bone smashing. FaceRep belongs on the soft side.
- **Face exercise clips.** #faceyoga has over 2 billion views. A 13-second face-yoga tutorial got 11.5 million. An
  "80 days of jaw exercises" before/after got 10.3 million views, and experts doubted the jaw changed and warned
  that hard daily jaw training can hurt the jaw joint. A "double chin gone in 57 days" video was debunked by a
  dentist and deleted.
- **A mascot that is a bit unhinged works.** Duolingo's owl took off by leaning into memes of it pestering people to
  do their lesson: entertain, don't sell, jump on trends fast, and post for months.

## What we take

1. Show the glow-up early: messy before, a swipe on the beat drop, the after. Then show how.
2. Let the big visible change be grooming, clothes, posture and sleep, which is what real glow-ups show anyway.
   FaceRep is the habit that starts it.
3. Use the trend's words and formats: "looksmax arc", "how it started vs how it's going", "POV", "the girlfriend
   effect".
4. Rep is our pushy mascot: he shows up, takes credit, guards the streak. Our own character, not a copy.
5. Short, saveable tutorials (one free exercise in about 13 s) as a second kind of post between stories.

## What we skip

Bone smashing, mewing, jaw-chewing gadgets, face ratings, "mogging" someone (putting another person down),
any claim that the jaw or face shape changed, and real streamers or creators.

## Hashtags

TikTok blocked looksmaxxing searches, so platforms may cool on the word. Keep "looksmax" in the video (it is the
hook) and pair it with #glowup #softmaxxing in the post text.

## Ideas from this

- `007-rep-effect`: the girlfriend-effect trend, with Rep taking the credit (script written).
- `004-day-30`: move the day-30 reveal to the beat drop at about 2 s, then show the days (waiting for the owner).
- A tutorial series, "free face workout in 13 seconds": one free exercise, the app's own steps, Rep counting.
- "Unhinged Rep": Rep pops up wherever Brody tries to skip (the fridge, the shower curtain, the game screen's glow)
